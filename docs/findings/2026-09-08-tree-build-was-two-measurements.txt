CORRECTED LATER THE SAME DAY BY P-194: the seven seconds are not established.
The same measurement repeated four times on a quiet machine spans 20.9 to
25.7 s on its own, so a single 20.9 against a single 27.9 sizes nothing, and
P-194's fill sweep finds no effect of database size at all up to 20,000
objects. See 2026-09-08-tree-build-fill-and-noise.txt. What stands below is
the structural half, which was never a measurement: the variants shared one
database and were not comparable with each other.

Two conclusions.

There is no per-request regression -- but the seven seconds are not a
measured quantity, see the correction above. The reasoning in this morning's
file is withdrawn there.

And the variance was never the problem: 0.6 s of spread over three samples. A
median would not have found this. The samples are worth their 55 seconds for
bounding the range, not for smoothing it.
